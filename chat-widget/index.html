Heal Buddy Widget

Look at the bottom-right corner of this page. Click the chat bubble to open the widget.

Embed on your site

Add this to your page (before </body>):

⚠️ Replace YOUR_GROQ_API_KEY with your actual key from console.groq.com/keys. 
<div id="heal-buddy-widget"></div>
<script src="https://yourdomain.com/widget.js"></script>
<script>
  HealBuddyWidget.init({
    apiKey: 'gsk_YOUR_API_KEY_HERE',
    avatars: {
      female: 'https://yourdomain.com/dr-sarah.png',
      male:   'https://yourdomain.com/dr-james.png',
      user:   'https://yourdomain.com/user.jpg'
    }
  });
</script>
